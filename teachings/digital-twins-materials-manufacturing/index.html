Principles of Digital Twins in Material Science and Manufacturing

Graduate Teaching Assistant. Course planning, grading criteria, office hours, and the Spectral Kit steel-plant simulation platform.

Instructor: Teaching Assistant

Term: Fall

Location: Carnegie Mellon University Africa, Kigali

My role

  • Helped create the course schedule for the semester and the grading criteria for assignments, labs and technical presentations.
  • Reviewed the key points and difficult parts of lectures with students, working through examples in detail.
  • Held office hours for students with less background in materials science and digital twins.
  • Set up Spectral Kit, a platform built on Docker and Streamlit that simulates a steel production plant, with sensor data collection and ML, physics and hybrid models.
