**April 2026**

- Developed an uncertainty-aware digital twin framework for real-time railway-crossing monitoring, using sensor-driven train-state estimation.
- Implemented predictive models and **MQTT-based distributed communication** to support safety decisions under noisy and uncertain sensor measurements.
- Built a **Streamlit dashboard** that shows predicted train position, uncertainty and confidence metrics, and crossing safety status in real time.
