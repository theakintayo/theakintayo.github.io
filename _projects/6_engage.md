**January – March 2024** · Team Lead

- Led a team of four from different backgrounds and nationalities, working on how engineering education is taught and practised in developing countries.
- Proposed **Engage**, a web-based platform that connects engineering stakeholders across the world to promote sustainable and inclusive growth. The proposal won the **People's Prize** at Reshaping Engineering 2024, run by Engineers Without Borders UK.
