**January 2026 – present** · Volunteer Researcher, AI HealthCare Research Laboratory, CMU-Africa

- Applied Random Forest classification to **Sentinel-2 imagery** and Rwanda land-cover data to characterise environmental land-cover patterns in a high malaria-risk region.
- Investigated associations between land-cover classes and malaria positivity rates to identify environmental differences between persistently high- and low-transmission areas.
